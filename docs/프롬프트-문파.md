# 문파 시트 프롬프트 — 문파 터 화면용 (v2.92 예정, 2026-09-16)

문파 탭을 열면 화면이 **내 문파 터**로 바뀐다(docs/설계-문파.md). 죽림 원경 위 땅에 전각 5채가 서고, 제자들이 마당에서 걷고 수련한다.
전각은 레벨에 따라 **빈 터 → 초가 → 기와집** 3단계로 자란다. 규격은 배경 소품(docs/프롬프트-지형.md)과 같다 — 옆모습·바닥선·마젠타.
시트가 나오면 자르지 말고 채팅에 그대로 붙여넣기(한 턴에 한 장).

## 1. 전각 5채 × 3단계 (한 장, 5줄 × 3칸)

줄 = 전각(연무장·장경각·약방·객당·산문), 칸 = 단계(터·초가·기와). 게임 배율은 주인공 키 47px 기준 — 기와집이 주인공 키의 2~2.5배(높이 100~120px)면
전각답다. 칸은 그 비율로 넉넉히(정사각 아님, 가로로 넓게).

```
2D pixel art building sprite sheet, 16-bit retro JRPG style, side view of ground structures, lit from the upper-left. A grid of 5 rows x 3 columns of thin dark-bordered cells of equal size, no empty cells. Each structure centered horizontally and standing on the BOTTOM edge of its cell, with a few pixels of margin from the top and sides so nothing is clipped (roof ridges and finials included). Solid magenta background (#FF00FF). No text, no labels, no signboard characters, no watermark. Crisp 1px black outlines, limited palette, clean readable silhouettes. Ancient Chinese wuxia martial-arts sect compound buildings. Each ROW is one building shown in three growth stages from left to right: column 1 = an empty plot with a few stakes, rope and a bamboo scaffold, column 2 = a humble thatched-roof wooden hut version, column 3 = a grand grey-tiled roof timber hall version with red pillars, upturned eaves and a stone base. Row 1: a martial training yard — a wooden wall with weapon racks (spears, staffs), a striking post (wooden dummy) and hanging sandbag in front. Row 2: a scripture library pavilion — two-story tower with lattice windows and a small bell. Row 3: an apothecary — a hut with drying herb bundles, a stone mortar and clay medicine jars. Row 4: a guest hall — a wide hall with an open front veranda, lanterns and a low tea table. Row 5: a mountain gate — a freestanding paifang gateway with two pillars and a roofed crossbeam, a plain blank name board (no characters). Muted green, warm timber brown, slate grey tile and deep red pillar palette matching a bamboo grove.
```

- 추출: 격자 검출(props_extract.py 계열) → 15장 `hall_<k>_<0|1|2>.png` (k = yard·library·clinic·guest·gate). 바닥 중앙 정렬, tight bbox, 규칙 0 눈검사.
- 데이터: SECT.halls[].sprite 규격은 review/hall_specs.json → 00-data. 단계 문턱은 SECT.stageLv (예: Lv 1 = 터, Lv 5 = 초가, Lv 20 = 기와).
- 패널 카드 액자(hall_<k>.png)는 기와 단계를 축소해 쓴다 — 별도 아이콘 시트 불필요.

## 2. 제자 — 걷기·수련 (한 장, 2줄 × 6칸)

계보 8종을 한 시트로: **살·머리는 고정**, **도복 색만** 코드가 계보색으로 물들인다(skinmatch 역방향 — 도복 마스크에 SCHOOLS 색). 그래서 시트의 도복은
**순회색(#9a9a9a) 단색 계열**로 요청한다. 키는 주인공보다 약간 작게(시트 안에서 비율만 — 배율은 추출 때 맞춘다).

```
2D pixel art character sprite sheet, 16-bit retro JRPG style, side view facing RIGHT, lit from the upper-left. A grid of 2 rows x 6 columns of thin dark-bordered cells of equal size, no empty cells, one full-body pose per cell, character centered and feet on the BOTTOM edge of the cell with margin so nothing is clipped. Solid magenta background (#FF00FF). No text, no watermark. Crisp 1px black outlines, limited palette. A young martial-arts disciple, slim, black hair tied in a topknot, plain NEUTRAL GREY training robe (#9a9a9a, flat grey with darker grey shading — the robe must be grey, no other hue), dark cloth belt, cloth shoes. Row 1: 6-frame walk cycle. Row 2: 6-frame training loop — a wooden practice sword (bokken) swung from raised guard down to a low finish and back. Same character proportions in every cell, no effects, no afterimages.
```

- 추출: hero_sheet 계열 gridless(균등 칸 + 발끝 땅). 결과 `disciple_walk.png`(6컷)·`disciple_train.png`(6컷). 도복 마스크(회색·저채도)는 추출 때 알파 채널 별도 PNG로 남겨 런타임에 계보색을 곱한다.
- 검증: spritetest 규격 + blackcheck + fxtest(터 화면에서 계보색 물들임이 실제로 그려지는지).

## 1b. 문파 터 배경 한 장 + 3/4 시점 전각 (사용자 제안 2026-09-16 "건물만 아이소메트릭으로, 배경도 따로")

진짜 아이소메트릭(30° 마름모)은 옆모습 인물·y 정렬 깊이와 어긋난다. **3/4 시점(살짝 위에서 내려다본 톱다운)**으로 한다 —
인물 스프라이트는 그대로 두고 배경·건물만 그 시점이면 흔한 2D RPG 마을 화면이 된다.

### 배경 (건물 없는 빈 마당, 1장 — 상단 원경처럼 폭 1024로 받아 화면 폭에 맞춰 줄인다)
```
2D pixel art background illustration, 16-bit retro JRPG style, three-quarter top-down view (slightly from above, like a classic 2D RPG town map), portrait orientation 9:16. A martial-arts sect courtyard in a bamboo grove: packed-earth training ground in the center with a few stone slabs and a cracked flagstone path running from the bottom edge up the middle; low mossy stone walls and a bamboo fence around the edges; tall bamboo stalks and a couple of pine trees along the top and sides; a small lotus pond with a stone lantern in one corner; a few rocks, grass tufts and fallen leaves. IMPORTANT: NO buildings, NO people, NO text — leave FIVE clearly flat, empty, open plots (bare earth) where buildings will be placed: two in the upper third (left and right), one in the upper-center, and two in the lower-middle (left and right). Muted green, olive, earth brown and grey palette, soft even daylight, no strong shadows, no border, no watermark.
```
- 추출 없이 통째로 쓴다(폭 1024 → 화면 폭). 다섯 빈 터의 화면 좌표를 재서 SECT.scene.halls에 적는다.
- **반영됨 (v2.92.6)** — 사용자 시트 572×1024 → `sheets/sect_bg.png` → `python sectbg.py` → assets/sect_bg(팔레트). 빈 터 5칸 = 뒷줄 장경각·객당(가운데)·약방, 앞줄 연무장·산문.
  터 폭이 그림의 22%(화면 ≈ 90~105px)라 옆모습 전각(기와 165px)은 터 폭×1.22에 맞춰 줄여 그린다 — 아래 3/4 시트가 오면 터 폭 기준으로 뽑는다(TARGET_W ≈ 110).

### 전각 5채 × 3단계 — 3/4 시점판, 배경에 맞춘 세세한 프롬프트 (v2.92.6 이후, 사용자 "건물 묘사 프롬프트 줘")

**배경 그림(sheets/sect_bg.png)을 참고 이미지로 같이 붙여서** 요청한다 — 톤·시점·선 굵기가 그림으로 전달돼야 어울린다. 빈 터가 가로로 넓은
직사각(가로:세로 ≈ 4:3, 그림 폭의 22%)이라 건물 발자국도 가로로 넓게. 배경 팔레트(실측): 잔디 #929852 · 맨땅 #9B865B · 이끼 돌담 #5B6E34 ·
돌길 #777860 · 연못 #4F7960. 5×3이 지저분하게 오면 **줄마다 한 장(1×3)** 으로 다섯 번 받는다(제미나이는 한 줄이 더 정확하다).

```
2D pixel art building sprite sheet for a retro 16-bit JRPG town map, drawn in EXACTLY the same style, palette, line weight and camera angle as the attached courtyard background: three-quarter top-down view (seen from slightly above and in front — the front wall with its door AND the whole roof are visible at once, roof ridge running left-right, front eave lower on screen than the back ridge), soft even daylight, no strong cast shadows, muted olive green / earth brown / mossy grey-green / warm timber palette (grass #929852, bare earth #9B865B, mossy stone #5B6E34, flagstone #777860), crisp 1px dark outlines, no dithering, no glow.

Layout: a grid of 5 rows x 3 columns of thin dark-bordered cells of equal size, all cells filled. ONE free-standing structure per cell, drawn as a single object on a solid magenta (#FF00FF) background, centered horizontally, its base (the ground it stands on) resting on the BOTTOM edge of the cell, with clear empty margin on the top, left and right so nothing is clipped. NO ground tile, NO grass patch under the building — the courtyard already has bare-earth plots; the structure must sit directly on magenta. No text, no labels, no signboard characters, no people, no watermark, no frame around the sheet. Every building's footprint is a WIDE landscape rectangle (about 4:3, wider than tall) that fills about 70% of the cell width, so all five halls come out at the same scale.

Each ROW is one building shown in three growth stages from left to right, same footprint and same position in all three:
  column 1 = the EMPTY PLOT stage: only a few wooden stakes with rope strung between them, one bamboo scaffold pole, a coiled rope and a small pile of timber — low, sparse, no walls.
  column 2 = the HUMBLE stage: a small single-story wooden hut with a straw THATCHED roof, mud-plaster walls, one door, one window — plain and rustic.
  column 3 = the GRAND stage: a stately timber hall with a grey-slate TILED roof, gently upturned eaves, dark red pillars, white plaster walls, a stone base — clearly bigger and richer than column 2 but still inside the same footprint.

Row 1 — TRAINING YARD (연무장): column 3 is an open-fronted pavilion (roof on red pillars, no front wall) with a wooden weapon rack of spears and staffs along the back, a wooden striking dummy (mu ren zhuang) and a hanging sandbag on a beam in front; column 2 is a thatched open shed with one rack and one post; column 1 is stakes plus a single practice post in bare earth.
Row 2 — SCRIPTURE LIBRARY (장경각): column 3 is a TWO-story tower hall, upper floor smaller than the lower, tiled roofs on both floors, lattice windows, a small bronze bell hanging under the front eave, a short stone stair to the door; column 2 is a one-story thatched hut with lattice windows and a bookshelf visible through the open door; column 1 is stakes and a stack of wooden crates.
Row 3 — APOTHECARY (약방): column 3 is a long low tiled hall with bundles of dried herbs hanging under the eave, a stone mortar, clay medicine jars and a drying rack of herbs on mats in front; column 2 is a thatched hut with two herb bundles and one jar; column 1 is stakes with a few herb mats on the ground.
Row 4 — GUEST HALL (객당): column 3 is the widest hall, with an open front veranda under the eave, two red paper lanterns hanging at the front corners, a low tea table with cups on the veranda, a wide doorway with double doors; column 2 is a thatched hut with a bench and a single lantern; column 1 is stakes and a low tea table on the bare earth.
Row 5 — MOUNTAIN GATE (산문): column 3 is a freestanding paifang gateway seen from the same angle — two dark red pillars, a tiled roofed crossbeam with upturned ends, a plain blank name board (no characters), a stone lion on each side; column 2 is a simple wooden torii-like gate of two posts and a thatched crossbeam; column 1 is two stakes with a rope strung between them.
```

- 추출: `halls.py` 그대로(덩어리 라벨링·줄 지우기). 배율은 **터 폭 기준**으로 바꾼다 — 기와 발자국 ≈ 화면 터 폭(≈100px)에 맞게 TARGET_W ≈ 110~120, hallFit 을 1.0 근처로.
- 검사: review/halls.png(바닥색 배경·주인공 자 포함) + 마당 스크린샷으로 시점·톤이 배경과 붙는지 본다. 톤이 뜨면 skinmatch 식으로 팔레트를 잔디·맨땅 쪽으로 당긴다.
