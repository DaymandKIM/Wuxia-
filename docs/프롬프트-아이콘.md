# 아이콘·카드 시트 프롬프트 — 다음에 그릴 것 (한 블록 = 한 장, 그대로 복사)

배경(원경·바닥·소품)은 5구역 다 들어갔다. 다음으로 게임에 필요한 그림 3종.
공통: 마젠타 `#FF00FF` 배경, 얇은 다크 테두리 칸, 한 칸에 하나, 텍스트·워터마크 없음,
보라·자주 금지(마젠타로 오인). 시트는 자르지 말고 채팅에 그대로.

## 1. 장비 아이콘 16종 — 다음 성장축(장비)용

무기 6 · 방어구 4 · 장신구 6. 등급 색은 게임이 테두리로 입히니 그림은 흰/회색 바탕 없이
**아이템 자체만**. 나중에 등급별 재요청 가능.

```
2D pixel art item icon sheet, 16-bit retro JRPG style, a grid of 16 equal square cells with thin dark borders, one item per cell, each item centered and filling about 70% of its cell, drawn at a consistent scale and angle (three-quarter view, tilted diagonally for weapons). Solid magenta background (#FF00FF). No text, no labels, no watermark, no purple or magenta tones in the items. Crisp 1px black outlines, limited palette, readable silhouettes at small size. Wuxia martial-arts equipment, one per cell in this order: row 1 = (1) a straight jian sword, (2) a curved dao saber, (3) a long spear, (4) a wooden staff, (5) iron-studded fist gauntlets, (6) an iron folding fan; row 2 = (7) a plain cloth martial robe, (8) a leather light armor vest, (9) a lamellar heavy armor, (10) a straw travel cloak; row 3 = (11) a jade pendant on a red cord, (12) a bronze ring, (13) a Buddhist prayer-bead bracelet, (14) a paper talisman, (15) a small gourd flask, (16) a silk hair ribbon. Muted steel, bronze, jade and cloth palette.
```

## 2. 기연 카드 일러스트 4종 — 기연 카드 배경 그림

카드 비율 3:4 세로, 한 칸에 하나. 글자는 게임이 얹는다(카드 아래 1/4은 비워둔다).

```
2D pixel art illustration sheet, 16-bit retro JRPG style, a row of 4 equal portrait cells (3:4 aspect) with thin dark borders, one illustration per cell, each filling the cell edge to edge with the bottom quarter kept simple and dark so text can be placed over it. Solid magenta (#FF00FF) only outside the cells. No text, no labels, no watermark, no purple or magenta tones. Crisp pixel shapes, atmospheric lighting, limited palette. Wuxia "fateful encounter" scenes: (1) an old tattered martial-arts manual lying open on a stone, glowing faintly; (2) a thousand-year spirit herb glowing in a misty cave; (3) a reclusive white-bearded hermit master sitting on a mountain rock at dusk, back half-turned; (4) a torn fragment of a secret manual page with a glowing seal. Warm lantern gold, ink black and mist grey palette.
```

## 3. 수련 스텟 아이콘 8종 — 수련 탭 타일용

```
2D pixel art icon sheet, 16-bit retro JRPG style, a grid of 8 equal square cells with thin dark borders, one flat symbolic icon per cell, centered, bold and readable at 24px, drawn in a matching simple style with a single accent color each. Solid magenta background (#FF00FF). No text, no watermark, no purple or magenta tones. Crisp 1px black outlines. Martial training stats, one per cell in this order: (1) a clenched fist (attack), (2) a heart with a lotus (health), (3) a swirling qi vortex (recovery), (4) a fist with motion lines (attack speed), (5) a pressure-point target with a needle (critical chance), (6) a cracked stone from a strike (critical damage), (7) a light-footed sandal with wind (move speed), (8) a stack of silver coins (silver gain). Warm gold, jade green, crimson and steel blue accents.
```
